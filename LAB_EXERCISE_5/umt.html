About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Its beginnings can be traced to the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia.

In June 1996, the campus became known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). The institution later developed into Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT), and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM received full university status and became Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Today, UMT offers programmes in marine science, fisheries, maritime studies, food science and technology.
